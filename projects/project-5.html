Background

Working within confidentiality

The project involved a real organisational challenge, but not everything from the work can be shared. Here I focus on the methods, design thinking and lessons I can discuss publicly.

Approach

Understanding the work behind the screens

I focused on the people using the system, the tasks they needed to complete and the places where the workflow became difficult to follow. Research and mapping helped make those problems clearer.

Process

From findings to interface ideas

The research informed flows and prototype concepts. My aim was to make the structure more straightforward, so people could focus on their task rather than work out where to go next.

Outcome

A process I can share responsibly

The final solution is not shared here. I can, however, explain my approach to research, design decisions and collaboration without exposing protected project details.

Behind the Design

Showing the process, respecting the boundaries

The examples below focus on methods and design thinking. Specific internal information and the final deliverable remain outside this public case study.

User Testing

User testing helped reveal needs, friction in the workflow and possible improvements. This case study focuses on the method rather than the protected final deliverable.

Affinity Mapping

Grouping observations into themes helped us see the issues that came up more than once.

Heuristic Evaluation

I reviewed the interface against usability principles to find issues with structure, clarity and interaction.

Prototype Concept

Developing interface concepts in Figma, with confidential details kept out of this presentation.

Key Learnings

Working with confidentiality

Good communication also means knowing what should stay private. I can explain a design decision without showing every artefact.

Real stakeholder context

An idea has to work within an organisation’s needs and constraints, not only for an individual user.

Structured UX process

Clear documentation helped connect each design direction to the research behind it.

Reflection

What I took from the project

This project taught me to make my reasoning clear, even when the finished work cannot be shown in full.

It also made me more careful about documenting decisions and sharing work responsibly in a professional setting.
